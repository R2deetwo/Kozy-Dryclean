KOZY CARE — BRAND KIT v6 README (WHAT CHANGED & HOW TO USE)
============================================================
One name. Three finishes. This update makes the name on every piece of
Kozy Care material match the website exactly, and adds two new finishes.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE NAME IS NOW ENFORCED — "Kozy Care", always two words
------------------------------------------------------------
Before this update, the LOGO FILES in the pack spelled the wordmark just
"KOZY" (with "PREMIUM DRY CLEANING" underneath), while the website and the
flyers said "Kozy Care". That is where the mixed naming came from.

What was fixed:
  - All logo lockups rebuilt as "Kozy Care" (title case — exactly the
    landing-page presentation) and "KOZY CARE" (tracked caps — the print
    presentation). True vector SVGs + high-res PNGs.
  - The monogram K is unchanged — it is an icon (favicons, avatars,
    embroidery, watermark), not the name, and it stays.
  - Brand Sheet V2 now opens with THE NAME section: the rules in one place
    (brand = Kozy Care; legal = Kozy Care Drycleaning & Laundry Services;
    bank = Kozy Cleaning Services Ltd; web = kozycare.ng; never "Kozy"
    alone in public-facing copy).

Nothing else about the approved navy look changed. The client liked the
current material — it is untouched in folder 01-current-kit-v5.1.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. THREE FINISHES — which material to use when
------------------------------------------------------------
SIGNATURE NAVY (the original, unchanged)
  Consumer / retail: flyers, posters, everyday customer-facing material.

LIGHT (new)
  The same approved flyer and poster re-set on Kozy Cream — for bright
  spaces, daylight notice boards, softer print runs. Gold text deepens to
  #B8942C on light grounds so it stays readable.

GOLD CORPORATE (new)
  For institutions and corporate buyers — hotels, restaurants, schools,
  hospitals, offices and the like. IMPORTANT: per the client's direction,
  these pieces never list those institution types. They speak the language
  those buyers already know — partnership review, scheduled collection,
  per-kilogram pricing, condition documentation, consolidated invoicing,
  dedicated account contact, HOTEL15 partner rates. Use the A4 services
  sheet for meetings and proposals; the A5 institutional flyer for visits
  and notice boards; the gold business cards at B2B meetings.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. FOR THE PRINTER (unchanged conventions)
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Card 85 x 55 mm + 3 mm bleed, crop marks included
  - Flyer A5 148 x 210 mm + 3 mm bleed; Poster A3 297 x 420 mm + 3 mm bleed
  - Corporate sheet A4 210 x 297 mm + 3 mm bleed
  - Gold = Pantone 871 C (or CMYK 0/17/74/17); Navy = CMYK 100/78/32/40
  - Pre-print checks: phone numbers current, prices match kozycare.ng,
    offers match admin Settings, both addresses correct.
