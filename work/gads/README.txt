KOZY CARE — GOOGLE ADS LOGO SUBMISSION KIT (v6.2 assets, unmodified copies)
============================================================================
Curated from the current brand kit (VERSIONS.txt: v6.2). No new artwork —
these are byte-for-byte copies of the existing logos (md5 verified).

WHERE EACH FILE GOES IN GOOGLE ADS
----------------------------------
SQUARE LOGO (Business profile / Business information → Logo)
  Google Ads wants 1:1, 120x120 to 1200x1200 px, PNG or JPG, not animated:
    kozy-app-icon-1024.png      1024x1024 (1:1) — THE square logo to submit
                                  (navy rounded square, gold K monogram)

LANDSCAPE LOGO (Business profile → Logo, landscape variant; some placements)
  Google Ads wants 4:1 (1200x300 recommended), PNG/JPG:
    kozy-logo-primary-navy.png  2400x600 (4:1) — navy lockup, white bg
    kozy-logo-primary-gold.png  2400x600 (4:1) — gold lockup, navy bg
    kozy-logo-primary-white.png 2400x600 (4:1) — white lockup, navy bg
    kozy-logo-compact-gold.png  2400x600 (4:1) — compact gold lockup, navy bg
    kozy-logo-print-caps-white.png 2400x600 (4:1) — print caps variant

SUBMIT THE FIRST TWO, KEEP THE REST AS ALTERNATES
-------------------------------------------------
  1. Square:   kozy-app-icon-1024.png
  2. Landscape: kozy-logo-primary-navy.png (recommended default — reads best
     on white). If Google rejects the navy version's contrast, try
     kozy-logo-primary-gold.png (light logo on dark bg) next.

The K monogram has been unchanged since v4; the lockups are the current
"Kozy Care" wordmark (v6). Do not use pre-v6 assets — they spell "KOZY".
