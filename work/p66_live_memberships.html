Quiet questions, honest answers

What counts as “couture” for The Atelier?

Designer pieces, aso-oke and lace, agbada, bridal and anything delicate enough that you would rather ask first. Atelier pieces are assessed before every treatment, hand-finished, and returned in protective covers. If you are unsure, send it with your next pickup — the studio will tell you which plan (or per-item care) suits it.

What if my bag is not full — or overflowing?

A half-full bag still counts as one of your pickups (the rider still rides). An overflowing one becomes an extra pickup — up to two a month at the plan rate, charged on that order. Nothing surprises you.

What happens to the bag or box if I leave?

It returns with your final delivery and that closes the chapter cleanly. If it does not come back, the replacement fee on your plan covers it — no deposits, no drama.

When do my duvets and curtains refresh?

Duvet and curtain perks reset every 3 months; the yearly deep clean resets each January. Your portal always shows what is left, so there is nothing to remember.

Can I really cancel any time?

Yes — one tap in your portal. The plan stays fully active to the last day you paid for, then simply does not renew. No calls, no forms, no drama.

How do I pay?

Card members are charged automatically each month through Paystack. Transfer members get a reminder before renewal with the studio account details — pay, attach the receipt, and the month extends.
