Quiet questions, honest answers

What if my bag is not full — or overflowing?

A half-full bag still counts as one of your pickups (the rider still rides). An overflowing one becomes your extra unit for the month — up to two extras at the plan rate, charged on that order, nothing surprises you.

What happens to the bag or box if I leave?

It returns with your final delivery and that closes the chapter cleanly. If it does not come back, the replacement fee on your plan covers it — no deposits, no drama.

When do my duvets and curtains refresh?

Quarterly perks reset with the calendar quarter; the annual spring clean resets each January. Your portal always shows what is left, so there is nothing to remember.

Can I really cancel any time?

Yes — one tap in your portal. The membership stays fully active to the last day you paid for, then simply does not renew. No calls, no forms, no retention theatre.

How do I pay?

Card members are charged automatically each month through Paystack. Transfer members receive a reminder before renewal with the studio account details — pay, attach the receipt, and the month extends.
