01 THE NAME
Kozy Care

The brand is always two words — Kozy Care — exactly as it appears on the website. Kozy alone never appears in customer-facing copy, signage or collateral. Capitalisation may follow the setting: title case Kozy Care (web, documents) or tracked caps KOZY CARE (print, labels). The monogram K is an icon — not the name.

06 RULES

DO

  • Write Kozy Care — both words, every time
  • Gold on navy; navy on cream or white; Deep Gold text on light grounds
  • Keep the clearspace margin around every lockup
  • Use supplied vector files (SVG / PDF) for print

DON'T

  • Never “Kozy” without “Care” in public-facing copy
  • Don't stretch, rotate, skew or re-colour the mark
  • Don't add shadows, gradients or outlines to the logo
  • Don't set light gold text on white — use Deep Gold or navy
