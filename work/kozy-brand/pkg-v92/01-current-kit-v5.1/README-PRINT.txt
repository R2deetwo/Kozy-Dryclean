----------------------------------------------------------------
PRINT SPECS (TELL THE PRINTER THIS)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Colour: CMYK (files are already converted). Large navy areas —
  rich navy build C100 M78 Y32 K40 (~250% total ink). If their press
  has a strict ink limit (e.g. 240% for offset), ask them to adjust
  the navy build; digital presses are fine as-is.
- Gold: prints as warm process gold (C0 M17 Y74 K17). For a TRUE
  metallic gold finish, ask for spot colour Pantone 871 C or hot-foil
  stamping — the artwork is flat solid gold, so it converts cleanly.
- Paper recommendation:
    Flyers      — 300 gsm silk or matte art card (lamination optional)
    Poster      — 250 gsm matte, or 170–200 gsm if being pasted
    Cards       — 350–400 gsm matte or soft-touch; navy card looks
                  premium with soft-touch lamination + gold foil on
                  the K mark (ask for Pantone 871 C foil)
- All text and logos are VECTOR in the PDFs — razor sharp at any size.
  Fonts are embedded.
- QR codes: navy on white. Flyers/poster min 20 x 20 mm as designed;
  business-card QR is 21 x 21 mm — still comfortably scannable.

----------------------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS CARDS — ADDING A PERSON'S NAME
----------------------------------------------------------------
The cards are brand-first (no personal name), so they work for the whole
team as-is. To add a name + title (e.g. on the back, above the contact
rows), open html-source/card-navy-back.html (or card-white-back.html),
find the comment-marked "headrow" block and add a line like:

    <div style="margin-top:2px;font-size:9px;color:#9FB0C6;letter-spacing:1px">
      YOUR NAME · FOUNDER</div>

then re-render (one command — see NEED AN EDIT below).
